Coping Techniques

4-7-8 Breathing Calm your nervous system instantly
5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Return to the present moment
Journaling Process emotions through writing
Mindfulness Build awareness, reduce stress

Breathing Exercise

Try 4-7-8 breathing: Inhale 4 sec · Hold 7 sec · Exhale 8 sec. Repeat 4× to activate your calm response.

Grounding — 5-4-3-2-1

Notice 5 things you see, 4 you touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. Anchors you in the present.

Self-Care Reminder

Drink water · Step outside 5 min · Write 3 things you're grateful for. Small acts compound over time.
